THE PROOF PATH

One message. Five explicit gates.

Each stage answers a separate question. Progress at one gate does not stand in for proof at the next.

  1. 01
    Source packetCanonical identity and inclusion evidence
  2. 02
    ConfirmationsConfigured source-chain depth
  3. 03
    Finalized policySemantic result only after finality
  4. 04
    Signer quorumIndependent authorization threshold
  5. 05
    Destination executionIdempotent delivery outcome

TRUST MODEL

Evidence is separated by responsibility.

Deterministic checks bind a message to its source packet, payload, pathway, confirmations, and replay state.

Semantic policy evaluates authorization meaning separately and must reach a finalized result before signing can begin.

Signer isolation is a target architecture for independent operators. This prototype does not claim a deployed production quorum.
